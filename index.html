Good to know

Questions before you start

Will the character move naturally throughout the video?

No. This offer uses distinct illustrated scenes with subtle zoom, transitions, and small effects. The cloud sample shows the expected style of motion.

Can I use a famous character or an existing logo as the reference?

Please share only material you have permission to use. A mood reference can guide tone, but this package does not copy an existing character or guarantee legal originality or trademark clearance.

What counts as the one minor revision?

A modest color, expression, short supplied wording, or timing change within the approved character and scene direction. A new character, new concept, or added scenes needs a separate scope and price.

Is the sample a client project?

No. The cloud friend is an in-house concept sample. There are no customer reviews, measured campaign results, or prior orders represented here.

How would I order?

This portfolio has no checkout. Prepare a brief below for your own use. If a Fiverr Gig is later published and approved, the final listing, platform requirements, and order timer will govern.
