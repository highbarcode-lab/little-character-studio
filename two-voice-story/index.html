The Dragon with a Little Voice작은 목소리의 용

진행법

  • 성인은 NARRATOR, 아이는 DRAGON을 읽어요.
  • 이름표 순서대로 번갈아 읽어요. 작은 목소리 그대로 읽어도 돼요.
  • 소품, 녹음, 계정 없이 두 사람과 이 대본으로 시작하세요.

두 역할로 읽기

성인 / NARRATOR

A little dragon carried a paper star up a windy hill. It belonged above the garden gate.

아이 / DRAGON

One small step.

성인 / NARRATOR

The gate opened when it heard a voice. The dragon tried to roar. Only a tiny sound came out.

아이 / DRAGON

Listen to my little voice.

성인 / NARRATOR

The wind swept the sound away. The gate stayed shut. The paper star shook in the dragon's paws.

아이 / DRAGON

I can try again.

성인 / NARRATOR

The dragon tried harder, but the wind was louder. The wind bent the paper star.

아이 / DRAGON

I'll try another way.

성인 / NARRATOR

Beside the gate stood a hollow wooden post. The dragon spotted a hole near its nose.

아이 / DRAGON

One small step.

성인 / NARRATOR

The dragon stepped behind the post, away from the wind, and leaned toward the hole.

아이 / DRAGON

Listen to my little voice.

성인 / NARRATOR

Quiet words travelled through the wood. The gate clicked and opened just a little.

아이 / DRAGON

I can try again.

성인 / NARRATOR

The dragon spoke again. Now the gate opened wide. Its little voice had arrived.

아이 / DRAGON

Listen to my little voice.

성인 / NARRATOR

Inside, the dragon placed the star above the gate. Warm light spread across the garden.

아이 / DRAGON

One small step.

성인 / NARRATOR

Outside, the wind still blew. The dragon's voice was still little. Inside, the star shone.

아이 / DRAGON

My little voice is enough.
