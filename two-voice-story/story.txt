The Dragon with a Little Voice / 작은 목소리의 용

진행법
성인은 NARRATOR, 아이는 DRAGON을 읽어요.
이름표 순서대로 번갈아 읽어요. 작은 목소리 그대로 읽어도 돼요.
소품, 녹음, 계정 없이 두 사람과 이 대본으로 시작하세요.

NARRATOR: A little dragon carried a paper star up a windy hill. It belonged above the garden gate.
DRAGON: One small step.

NARRATOR: The gate opened when it heard a voice. The dragon tried to roar. Only a tiny sound came out.
DRAGON: Listen to my little voice.

NARRATOR: The wind swept the sound away. The gate stayed shut. The paper star shook in the dragon's paws.
DRAGON: I can try again.

NARRATOR: The dragon tried harder, but the wind was louder. The wind bent the paper star.
DRAGON: I'll try another way.

NARRATOR: Beside the gate stood a hollow wooden post. The dragon spotted a hole near its nose.
DRAGON: One small step.

NARRATOR: The dragon stepped behind the post, away from the wind, and leaned toward the hole.
DRAGON: Listen to my little voice.

NARRATOR: Quiet words travelled through the wood. The gate clicked and opened just a little.
DRAGON: I can try again.

NARRATOR: The dragon spoke again. Now the gate opened wide. Its little voice had arrived.
DRAGON: Listen to my little voice.

NARRATOR: Inside, the dragon placed the star above the gate. Warm light spread across the garden.
DRAGON: One small step.

NARRATOR: Outside, the wind still blew. The dragon's voice was still little. Inside, the star shone.
DRAGON: My little voice is enough.
